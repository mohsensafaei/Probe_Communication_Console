# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully, regardless of experience, background
or identity. Discuss code and evidence, ask questions in good faith and accept
constructive disagreement. Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discrimination,
unwanted sexual attention and publishing private information are not acceptable.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related
community interactions. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and warn,
restrict or ban participants according to the impact and persistence of the behavior.
Decisions should be proportionate, consistent and protect affected people.

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through a private contact listed on
[the maintainer's profile](https://github.com/mohsensafaei). Do not publish private
incident details in an issue. If no private route is listed, use GitHub's reporting
controls. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not adjudicate it alone.
